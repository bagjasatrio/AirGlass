Built to replace three frustrating walls

Most Windows receivers are paid, capped, or break when headphones unplug. AirGlass solves each issue at the layer where the protocol actually operates.

  • Trial builds that expire mid-presentation

    Fifteen-minute timers and intrusive watermarks. AirGlass has no clock, no account, and no upsell. The codebase is licensed under GPL-3 and remains fully open.

  • Smeared video and CPU thermal spikes

    Frames pass directly to hardware decode on your GPU, then render through an NV12-to-RGB Direct3D 11 shader. When hardware decode is unavailable, frame rates cap honestly instead of silently compressing resolution.

  • Audio that drifts or vanishes

    A WASAPI event-driven audio sink consumes 20ms buffers directly. Video presentation deadlines synchronize to the audio clock. Dropping a video frame is allowed; dropping audio is not.

Choose quality before you mirror

AirGlass probes your GPU, CPU cores, and Wi-Fi band before starting, displaying real capability verdicts. Resolutions quote the short side of portrait orientation (1080p is approximately 886 by 1920 pixels).

  • 1080p30

    Default

    Sharp text rendering at 30 fps. Runs reliably on integrated graphics over standard 5 GHz Wi-Fi.

  • 1080p60

    Full 60 fps fluidity for gaming and fast UI animations. Requires hardware decode (D3D11VA) and 5 GHz Wi-Fi.

  • 720p60

    Designed for older dual-core CPUs, legacy laptops, and 2.4 GHz wireless networks.

A clean capture window for OBS and streaming

Standard capture tools show the full receiver interface, buttons, and sidebars. AirGlass places the mirrored screen inside an independent borderless window titled AirGlass Mirror.

  • Borderless window output captures purely the mobile screen inside OBS Studio or Discord.
  • Freely resizable from any edge or corner, and draggable across monitors.
  • When your iPhone screen turns off, the session remains active and displays a clean black frame until the device wakes up.

Lossless AirGlass Audio Player

Stream music, podcasts, and video audio from your iOS device directly to your PC speakers over Wi-Fi without Bluetooth compression artifacts.

  • Delivers 44.1 kHz / 16-bit lossless PCM audio with sub-20ms latency via WASAPI.
  • Parses DAAP metadata containers (track title, artist, album, and duration) from Apple Music and supported apps.
  • Falls back gracefully to a clean live audio stream when third-party apps like Spotify or YouTube stream raw PCM without ID3 tags.
  • Independent tab navigation keeps your active workspace open when an audio session connects.

Two-Way Web Drop over local Wi-Fi

Move photos, 4K videos, and documents between iOS and Windows without cloud uploads, cables, or iTunes installations.

  • Scan the desktop QR code with your iPhone camera to open the local Safari transfer portal instantly.
  • Send files from PC to iPhone using the Send Files button, staged safely in AppData without polluting work directories.
  • Custom save destination lets you choose where received files land on your PC.
  • Background keep-alive uses the Screen Wake Lock API and a silent video loop with 3x auto-retry so large file uploads survive screen locks.

Protocol boundaries and technical limits

  • No remote phone control

    AirPlay is an output streaming protocol. It carries no touch or mouse injection signals back to iOS.

  • No DRM playback

    FairPlay Streaming and HDCP copy protection render video frames black by Apple protocol design.

  • Sender capped near 1080p

    iOS AirPlay encoders deliver up to 1080p on the short edge. The resolution limit is set by iOS, not Windows.

  • Unsigned binary

    The build is unsigned. Windows SmartScreen displays a one-time notice on first run (click More Info, then Run Anyway).
