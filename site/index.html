Built against three things that annoyed me

Most Windows receivers are paid, capped, or both. These are the three failures AirGlass was written to remove, each fixed at the layer where it actually breaks.

  • Trial builds that stop mid-scene

    Fifteen minute caps and watermarks. AirGlass has no clock, no account and no upsell, because the licence underneath is GPL-3 and always will be.

  • Soft, smeared picture

    Frames go through hardware decode when the GPU offers it, then a direct NV12 to RGB shader. When hardware decode is missing, the frame rate is capped honestly instead of the resolution being quietly reduced.

  • Audio that arrives late

    A WASAPI event-driven sink feeds 20 ms buffers, and video is presented against the audio position. Dropping a video frame is allowed. Dropping audio is not.

Pick the preset before you mirror

AirGlass probes your GPU, CPU and Wi-Fi band, then shows what this machine can hold. You choose. Nothing is decided silently, and the choice stays locked for that session. Sizes are quoted as the short side of the portrait screen, so 1080p is roughly 886 by 1920 in practice.

  • 1080p30

    Default

    Holds on integrated graphics over a decent 5 GHz link. The safest way to start.

  • 1080p60

    Smoothest motion, and the first preset to stutter when hardware decode is unavailable.

  • 720p60

    For older laptops, 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi and CPU-only decoding. Frame rate without the resolution.

Auto

Lets the probe pick for you, once, before the session starts. It is locked the same way as the others.

A window with nothing but the phone

Capture windows usually drag the whole application with them: sidebar, buttons, footer. AirGlass puts the mirrored screen in a second window with no chrome at all.

  • Borderless, so a window capture shows only the phone.
  • Drag it anywhere, resize from any edge.
  • When the iPhone screen sleeps, the session stays alive and the window goes black until it wakes.

Graphics
Any GPU with Direct3D 11 feature level 10_0. Hardware decode is used when present
Network
iPhone and PC on the same Wi-Fi network. 5 GHz recommended for 1080p
Disk
About 120 MB for the portable folder, no installer

First run

  1. ExtractThe zip holds the executable and the DLLs it needs. Keep them in the same folder.
  2. RunStart AirGlass, allow it through the firewall prompt on private networks, then open Control Center on the iPhone and pick Screen Mirroring.
  3. Choose a presetPick the resolution and frame rate once, at the start. The receiver advertises exactly that.

What it does not do

  • It does not control the phone

    This is a receiver. Taps and typing on the PC do not reach iOS.

  • It cannot show DRM video

    Protected playback stays black, by protocol design rather than by choice.

  • It is not 4K

    AirPlay from iOS tops out near 1080p on the short side. The ceiling is the sender, not the app.

  • It is unsigned

    There is no code signing certificate behind this build. SmartScreen warns once, then remembers.
